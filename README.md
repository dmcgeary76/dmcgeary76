## Featured

### [K-12 AI Legislative Map](https://dmcgeary76.github.io/ailegmap/)
An interactive U.S. map that tracks K-12 AI legislation state by state. Bills sync from LegiScan and pass through a human review queue before they show up on the map, and each state also gets a hand-researched profile of its guidance. The goal is an honest picture of how schools are actually being told to handle AI, not just the headlines.

## What I'm thinking about

AI in classrooms is arriving faster than anyone is thinking through what it does to kids. I'm most interested in **responsible AI in education**, which to me means designing for how students actually use these tools rather than how we intended them to. It also means being honest about what an AI is and what it won't remember. A tutor that seems to know a child this year but forgets them next year raises a design problem as well as an ethical one.
